How to Swim?

Getting Started!

Swimming is a vital skill that offers numerous benefits, from fitness to safety. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to refine your technique, understanding the basics is essential. There are a lot of ways to keep yourself floating in the water: using a kickboard, practicing your strokes, and learning to relax are all effective methods.

Swimming Essentials

  • Swimsuit
  • Goggles
  • Swim cap
  • Kickboard
  • Towel
