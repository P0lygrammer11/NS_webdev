Zion National Park

Explore Utah's Towering Red Cliffs & Emerald Canyons

Why Visit Zion?

Located in southwestern Utah near the town of Springdale, Zion National Park is a world-famous vacation destination that draws millions of travelers from around the globe every year. Towering 2,000-foot Navajo sandstone cliffs rise dramatically above the lush, tree-lined Virgin River floor, creating scenery you cannot find anywhere else on Earth. Whether you are seeking heart-pounding cliffside hikes or peaceful scenic drives through red rock tunnels, Zion offers an unforgettable outdoor adventure for every type of traveler.

Top 4 Reasons to Go

  • Hike The Narrows: Wade directly through the cool waters of the Virgin River inside a narrow slot canyon with walls soaring a thousand feet overhead.
  • Conquer Angels Landing: Experience one of America's most famous trails, featuring steep switchbacks and breathtaking panoramic views of Zion Canyon.
  • Spot Diverse Wildlife: Keep your eyes peeled for desert bighorn sheep scaling steep rock faces, mule deer grazing by the river, and California condors soaring above.
  • Stargaze Under Dark Skies: As a certified International Dark Sky Park, Zion offers crystal-clear views of the Milky Way far away from city light pollution.
